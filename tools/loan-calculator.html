How EMI is calculated

This uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly instalments. Figures are estimates — your lender's exact terms may differ.
