Word (.docx) to PDF

Convert a .docx file to PDF, right in your browser. Works best for text documents with normal headings, paragraphs and lists.

This converts the document's text and basic formatting (headings, bold/italic, lists, simple tables). Complex layouts — multi-column pages, text boxes, headers/footers, or heavily styled tables — may not carry over exactly. Old .doc files aren't supported, only .docx.
