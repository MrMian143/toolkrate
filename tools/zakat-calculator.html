How this is worked out

Nisab is commonly set at the value of 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver — whichever is lower, using the silver standard, is the more cautious and widely-used choice, so that's what this tool applies. Zakat is 2.5% of total zakatable wealth once your net assets (assets minus due debts) meet or exceed the nisab threshold and a lunar year has passed on that wealth. This is a general calculation; for your specific situation it's best to confirm with a knowledgeable scholar.
