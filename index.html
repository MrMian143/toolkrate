Why ToolKrate

Every tool on this site runs as plain JavaScript in your own browser. There's no upload step and no server processing your input, which means your text, numbers and files stay on your device the whole time. That also makes the tools fast — results update as you type, with no waiting on a network request.

New tools get added as they're built. If there's a small calculator or converter you keep needing and can't find here, the contact page is the place to ask for it.
